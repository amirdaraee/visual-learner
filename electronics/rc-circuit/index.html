The RC circuit

A battery, a switch, a resistor and a capacitor in one loop. The switch either connects the battery to the resistor (charge) or connects the resistor across the capacitor alone (discharge). The time constant τ = R × C sets the pace of both.

Reading the scene

Amber dots are conventional current. Their speed follows the current, compressed so small currents stay visible. The cyan fill between the capacitor plates shows its voltage. The screen on the board plots the same curve as the card.
