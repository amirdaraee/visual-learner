Your privacy

Visual Learner has no accounts, no ads and nothing for sale. It counts visits so I can see which topics are useful. Here is exactly what that involves.

Always on: Cloudflare Web Analytics

Page views are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or local storage for this and does not track people across sites. What it records for each view: the page address, the referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type.

Only if you accept: Google Analytics

A banner asks once. If you click Accept, Google Analytics 4 loads and sets cookies named _ga and _ga_…. It sends page views and which steps of a topic you open, with approximate location and device details, to Google. Advertising features and Google signals are switched off. If you click Decline, Google Analytics is never loaded and no Google cookie is set.

Do Not Track

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, neither tool loads and you will not see the banner.

Other things your browser does

  • Fonts and libraries. The fonts come from Google Fonts, and the 3D library from cdnjs and jsDelivr. Those providers see your IP address and browser details when the files load. This happens whatever you choose above.
  • Saved in your browser. Your choice here, and whether you muted sound or turned off effects or the backdrop. These stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.
