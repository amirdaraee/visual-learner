Waves from circles

A Fourier series writes a repeating wave as a sum of sine waves at the base frequency and its whole-number multiples (the harmonics). A point going round a circle at steady speed has a height that is a sine wave, so each harmonic is a circle, and the circles are chained: each one is centred on the rim of the one before.

Recipes

Square: odd harmonics k, amplitude 4/(πk). Sawtooth: every harmonic n, amplitude 2/(πn), alternating sign. Triangle: odd harmonics k, amplitude 8/(π²k²), alternating sign.
