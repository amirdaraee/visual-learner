Two ripples

Two sources shake the water at the same rate and in step. At every point the two waves add. The result depends on the path difference Δ = |r₁ − r₂| in wavelengths: whole numbers add up, half-odd numbers cancel.

What is simplified

The tank is a schematic water surface. Heights are exaggerated, lengths are in arbitrary tank units (u), the wave speed is held fixed, and the edges absorb the waves instead of reflecting them. Real water ripples also lose height to friction and travel at a speed that depends on wavelength.
